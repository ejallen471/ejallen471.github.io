Projects

Below you will find a selection of my projects from my undergraduate and masters degrees. Please get in touch if you have any questions

Selected Works

NeuralGCM: Hybrid Physics-AI for Weather and Climate. With Additional Tropical Cyclone Case Studies

In 2026, I completed my MPhil project asking whether NeuralGCM is a good weather and climate model. I reproduced results from the original NeuralGCM paper, then tested its ability to capture the tracks, intensity and distinctive dynamics of three tropical cyclone case studies. The results show that NeuralGCM is a strong hybrid physics and AI model, but still struggles with cyclone intensity and complex dynamics. My project was supervised by Professor Sebastian Schemm, Dr Simon Driscoll and Pritthijit Nath.

High Dimensional PDF Covariance Reconstruction via Kernel Density Estimation

In summer 2025 I completed a research project based on analysing Gaussianity in NNPDF4.0 replicas and non parametric reconstruction of Parton Distribution Functions using Kernel Density Estimation. My work was supervised by Professor Luigi Del Debbio.

The Physics of Morphologically Diverse Bacteria in Moving Fluids

From September to November 2024, I completed my senior honours project investigating how the curvature of a single bacterium affects its rotational dynamics in shear flow. My project was supervised by Dr. Tyler N. Shendruk and Dr. Gavin Melaugh.

Solar System Simulation

I created this project as part of the Computer Simulation course at the University of Edinburgh in my second year. The project aims were to visualise the planetary orbits of the solar system and conduct different experiments related to energy conservation and integration techniques.
