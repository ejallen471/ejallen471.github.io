Meringues

← Back to Baking

A simple meringue recipe using the ratio: 50g caster sugar for every egg white.

  • Oven Temperature140°C fan assisted
  • Baking45 minutes to 2 hours
  • MakesDepends on size

Ingredients

  • 1 egg white
  • 50g caster sugar

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 140°C fan assisted and line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Whisk the egg white until foamy and starting to hold soft peaks.
  3. Add the caster sugar a little at a time while continuing to whisk.
  4. Keep whisking until the meringue is thick, glossy and holds stiff peaks. It should be firm enough that you can turn the bowl upside down without it falling out.
  5. Spoon or pipe the meringue onto the lined baking tray.
  6. Bake until the meringues are set and peel away from the baking paper easily. Depending on their size, this can take around 45 minutes for small meringues or up to a couple of hours for larger ones.

Notes

  • If needed, wipe the mixing bowl with a teaspoon of vinegar before starting.
  • For giant meringues with a crisp outside and gooey middle, reduce the temperature to 120°C fan assisted and bake for around 6 hours.
  • For large batches of more than about 5 egg whites, gently warm the sugar in the oven before adding it. This helps it dissolve into the egg whites.

Variations

  • For coloured meringues, paint food colouring inside the piping bag before adding the meringue.
  • For chocolate meringues, fold in 1 tsp cocoa powder at the end, before piping or spooning onto a tray.
