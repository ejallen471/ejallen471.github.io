Giant Chocolate Fondant with Salted Caramel Centre

← Back to Baking

This is a family favourite at Christmas time. To make it extra special, we make it giant! Use an oven-proof bowl for baking the chocolate sponge and a ramekin for making the salted caramel centre.

This recipe is adapted from BBC Food's chocolate fondant with caramel centre.

  • Oven Temperature200°C fan assisted, then 180°C fan assisted
  • Baking30 minutes
  • ServesUp to 6 people

Ingredients

Caramel Centre

  • 400ml whipping cream
  • 200g soft light brown sugar
  • 35g soft muscovado sugar
  • 70g butter
  • Pinch of pepper
  • 1/2 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt, to taste

Chocolate Fondant

  • 30g cocoa powder
  • 30g caster sugar
  • 80g dark chocolate
  • 80g milk chocolate
  • 160g butter, plus extra for greasing
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 90g icing sugar
  • 30g arrowroot
  • Pinch of salt

Method

Make the Salted Caramel Centre

  1. Put all the caramel ingredients in a pan and warm gently until the sugar has dissolved.
  2. Turn up the heat and let the caramel bubble for 2-3 minutes, until it looks golden and syrupy.
  3. Leave it to cool, then taste it and adjust the salt, lemon juice and pepper until it tastes right.
  4. Line a ramekin with baking paper, pour in the caramel and freeze it overnight.

Make the Chocolate Fondant

  1. Grease an oven-proof bowl with butter, then dust it with the cocoa powder and caster sugar.
  2. Melt the dark chocolate, milk chocolate and butter together in a heatproof bowl set over a pan of simmering water. Set it aside to cool slightly.
  3. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan assisted.
  4. Put the eggs and egg yolks in a bowl and whisk until pale and fluffy.
  5. Sift in the icing sugar and whisk until combined.
  6. Sift in the arrowroot and keep whisking until the mixture is pale, thick and doubled in volume. When you lift the beaters, they should leave a trail on the surface.
  7. Fold in the melted chocolate mixture until everything is combined.

Assemble and Bake

  1. Fill the greased bowl three-quarters full with the chocolate mixture.
  2. Add the frozen caramel centre, then cover it with the remaining chocolate mixture.
  3. Bake for 10 minutes at 200°C fan assisted, then reduce the temperature to 180°C fan assisted and bake for a further 20 minutes.
  4. Let the fondant cool for 5 minutes, then run a butter knife around the outside and turn it out onto a serving plate.
