Fig, Almond and Ricotta Cake

← Back to Baking

A soft ricotta, almond and orange cake topped with fresh figs, flaked almonds, demerara sugar and honey.

  • Oven Temperature170°C fan assisted
  • Baking35-45 minutes
  • Serves8-10

Ingredients

Wet Ingredients

  • 250g ricotta
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 100ml extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 2 tbsp orange juice

Dry Ingredients

  • 180g ground almonds
  • 120g plain flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp sea salt

To Finish

  • 6-8 fresh figs, halved
  • 2 tbsp flaked almonds
  • 1 tbsp demerara sugar
  • Squeeze of honey

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 170°C fan assisted.
  2. Line a cake tin with baking paper, or line it with fig leaves if you have them.
  3. Mix the ricotta, caster sugar, olive oil, eggs, vanilla, orange zest and orange juice together in a bowl.
  4. In a separate bowl, mix the ground almonds, plain flour, baking powder and sea salt.
  5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.
  6. Spoon the batter into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
  7. Arrange the halved figs on top, then scatter over the flaked almonds and demerara sugar.
  8. Bake for 35-45 minutes, until the cake is golden and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
  9. Finish with a squeeze of honey while the cake is still warm.
