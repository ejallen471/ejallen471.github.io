Fig Leaf Cordial

← Back to Baking

A fragrant fig leaf cordial made by steeping fresh fig leaves, then reducing the liquid with sugar.

  • MakesAbout 500ml

Ingredients

  • 10 or more fig leaves
  • 500g caster sugar
  • 1 litre water
  • Peel of 1 lemon
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Method

  1. Wash the fig leaves well and dry them.
  2. Boil 1 litre of water.
  3. Add the fig leaves, lemon peel and lemon juice to the hot water, then leave them to steep for 24 hours.
  4. Remove the leaves and lemon peel.
  5. Add the caster sugar to the fig leaf water and heat until dissolved.
  6. Simmer until the liquid has reduced by half.
  7. Pour into sterilised bottles for storage.

Variations

  • Use more leaves for a stronger cordial, and adapt the amount to your preference.
  • You can also toast the leaves gently first. Place them in a pan over the heat until they start to curl, then remove and add them to the water to steep.
