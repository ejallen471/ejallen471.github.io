Swiss Meringue Buttercream

← Back to baking

A smooth, light buttercream for filling and decorating cakes.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg white
  • 75g caster sugar
  • 100g butter, softened and cut into small pieces

Method

  1. Place the egg white and caster sugar in a heatproof bowl over a pan of gently simmering water. Make sure the base of the bowl does not touch the water.
  2. Stir continuously until the sugar has completely dissolved. Rub a little mixture between your fingers. It is ready when it feels completely smooth, without any grains of sugar.
  3. Remove the bowl from the heat and whisk until the meringue is glossy and stiff and the bowl feels cold. It should be firm enough to hold above your head.
  4. Add the softened butter one piece at a time, whisking for about 10 seconds before adding the next piece.
  5. The buttercream will become thinner as the butter is added. Keep whisking and it will thicken into a smooth buttercream.

Notes

Multiply the ingredients evenly to make as many batches as needed. For a three layer cake, use 4 egg whites, 300g caster sugar and 400g butter.

The buttercream keeps well in the fridge and can also be frozen. Allow chilled or frozen buttercream to return to room temperature, then whisk it again until smooth before using.
